# Constraints: socket_housing

## Functional Requirements
- House a standard E26 light bulb socket.
- Provide a mounting surface for the lamp shade.
- Connect to the stem.

## Dimensions
- Overall: 45mm outer diameter x 60mm height.
- Internal bore: 38mm diameter to a depth of 42mm to fit a standard E26 socket. This provides enough material for the threaded hole.
- Bottom hole: Tapped for M12x1.5 thread to a depth of 18mm.
- Shade seating rim: A 45mm outer diameter flange at the top. The resulting flange width will be 3.5mm based on the OD and ID.

## Features
- Cylindrical shape.
- Large internal bore for the socket.
- Tapped hole in the base.
- A flange for the shade to rest on.

## Interfaces
- Connects to `stem` via M12x1.5 thread.
- `shade` rests on the top flange.
- Houses a standard E26 socket (press-fit or set screw).

## Manufacturing
- Primary process: CNC turning
- Material class: Aluminum 6061
- Secondary processes: Tapping (M12x1.5)
