# Constraints: base

## Functional Requirements
- Provide a stable foundation for the lamp.
- Must be heavy enough to prevent tipping.
- Allow for power cord routing.

## Dimensions
- Overall: 150mm diameter x 20mm height.
- Central hole: Tapped for M12x1.5 thread to a depth of 15mm.
- Cord channel: 6mm wide x 6mm deep channel from the central hole to the outer edge.
- Pad recess: 140mm diameter x 2mm deep recess on the bottom face.

## Features
- Circular shape.
- Fillet on the top outer edge with a 2mm radius.
- Central tapped hole for the stem.
- Recess for a rubber anti-slip pad.

## Interfaces
- Connects to `stem` via a threaded M12x1.5 interface.
- Sits on the desk surface, interfaces with a rubber pad.

## Manufacturing
- Primary process: CNC milling
- Material class: Aluminum 6061
- Secondary processes: Tapping (M12x1.5)
