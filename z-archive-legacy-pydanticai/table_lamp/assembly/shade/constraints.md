# Constraints: shade

## Functional Requirements
- Diffuse and direct light from the bulb.
- Must not overheat from the bulb's heat output.
- Seat securely on the socket housing.

## Dimensions
- Overall: 120mm outer diameter (at base) x 100mm height.
- Top opening: 60mm diameter.
- Bottom opening: 120mm diameter.
- Seating interface: An internal diameter of 45.5mm to fit onto the socket housing flange. Wall thickness of 3mm.

## Features
- Conical or cylindrical shape.
- Open top and bottom.
- A defined rim on the inside of the bottom opening to locate it on the socket housing.

## Interfaces
- Rests on the `socket_housing` flange.

## Manufacturing
- Primary process: CNC milling
- Material class: Aluminum 6061
- Secondary processes: Anodizing (optional, for color)
- Note: While spinning is common for shades, the goal is CNC milling. This will require a 3+2 or 5-axis approach.
