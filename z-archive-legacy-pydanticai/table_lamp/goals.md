# Design Goals

Design a desk table lamp with these parts: (1) A weighted circular base with a central hole for the neck pipe, rubber pad recess on bottom. (2) A cylindrical lamp shade / diffuser with an open bottom rim that seats onto the socket housing. (3) A light bulb with an Edison E26 screw base (threaded cylinder) and a glass envelope (simplified as a smooth solid of revolution). All parts should be designed for CNC milling except the bulb which is for reference/visualization only. The assembly should show the bulb screwed into the socket area of the shade, mounted on the base.
