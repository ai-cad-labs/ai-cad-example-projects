# Constraints: tire

## Functional Requirements
- Provides traction between the vehicle and the road surface.
- Supports the vehicle load.
- Absorbs road shocks.

## Dimensions
- Tread Width: 225mm
- Inner Diameter: To fit a 17-inch (432mm) wheel rim.

## Features
- Standard automotive tire bead for sealing against the wheel rim.

## Interfaces
- Mounts to the `alloy_wheel` rim. The interface is an interference fit, held by air pressure.

## Manufacturing
- Primary process: Rubber molding
- Material class: Rubber composite
- Note: This is a standard part to be purchased, not manufactured.
