# Design Goals

A car wheel assembly: 1) Wheel hub - cylindrical hub (80mm OD, 40mm bore, 30mm thick) with a 5-bolt circle pattern (M12 bolts on 100mm PCD). CNC machined aluminum. 2) Alloy wheel - 17-inch (432mm OD) 5-spoke pentagonal design with a central bore (80mm) matching the hub, 5 bolt holes matching the hub pattern, and a rim flange for the tire. CNC machined aluminum. 3) Tire - rubber tire, 225mm tread width, fits on the wheel rim. Note: tire is rubber molding, no CNC rules apply. Assembly: hub + wheel + tire with 5 M12 bolts.
