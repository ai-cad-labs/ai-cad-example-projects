# Spur Gear Pair with Shaft
Design a meshing spur gear pair mounted on two parallel shafts:
- Pinion: involute spur gear, module 2 mm, 18 teeth, 20° pressure angle, 10 mm face width, central bore ⌀8 mm for the shaft
- Gear: involute spur gear, module 2 mm, 30 teeth, 20° pressure angle, 10 mm face width, central bore ⌀8 mm for the shaft
- Shaft: plain cylindrical shaft ⌀8 mm × 30 mm long — ONE part, used twice (one shaft per gear)
- Meshing requirement: pinion and gear must mesh — pitch diameters 36 mm and 60 mm, centre distance = (36+60)/2 = 48 mm; the involute teeth must interlock at the pitch line without interpenetration
- Both gears sit at the same axial position (coplanar), axes parallel along Z, separated 48 mm; each shaft passes through its gear's bore
- Manufacturing process: 3D_printing
- Parts must fit together as an assembly (pinion + gear meshed on parallel shafts at 48 mm centre distance); export STEP + STL for every part once validated
